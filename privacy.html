Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026. This policy covers loopnode.live and every LoopNode tool subdomain, including car.loopnode.live and taqaud.loopnode.live.

What you type into a tool

  • taqaud.loopnode.live (Saudi retirement calculator): everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored.
  • car.loopnode.live (car-loan interest deduction checker): only the VIN leaves your browser, and only when you ask for a check. It goes straight to the public VIN decoder run by the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), not to us. Your answers, loan figures and income stay in your browser.
  • No tool has accounts, sign-in or a database. Nothing is kept after you close the page.

This hub

loopnode.live itself runs no scripts and has no forms. It only shows the pages you are reading.

Hosting logs

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data about each visit, such as your IP address, for security and to run the service. See the GitHub privacy statement. These logs never contain what you type into a tool.

Cookies and analytics

Today, no LoopNode site sets cookies of its own or runs analytics.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We plan to show ads through Google AdSense on LoopNode sites to pay for them. When ads are switched on:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: Google's partner-sites policy.
  • Where the law requires consent before personalised ads (for example in the EEA and the UK), a consent message is shown first.

Ads never see what you type into a tool: that stays in your browser, except the VIN described above, which goes only to NHTSA.

Your rights

We do not collect personal data about you, so there is nothing we hold to show, correct or delete. For any privacy question, contact us.

Changes

If anything above changes — for example when ads are switched on — we update this page and its date first.

LoopNode makes independent tools. We are not affiliated with any government agency; results are estimates, not advice.
