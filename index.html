LoopNode Tools

Small, free web tools that answer one question well — and show their working.

Each tool does one job for one audience, in their language, on its own address. It runs in your browser, quotes the official rule it applies, and tells you the date we last checked that rule. When a question needs an official decision, the tool says who makes it.

How we build them

  • Your data stays with you. The tools calculate on your device. The Saudi retirement calculator sends nothing anywhere. The car checker sends one thing — the VIN you type — straight from your browser to the public VIN decoder run by the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration; it never reaches us.
  • Every rule has a source. We copy rules and tables from the official pages that publish them, keep the exact wording, and show the link and the date we checked it next to the result.
  • The official examples are our tests. Before a tool is released, it has to reproduce the worked examples that the agency itself publishes. If it cannot, it does not ship.
  • What we could not verify, we leave out. If a rule is not stated on a primary source we can read, the tool says so instead of guessing.
  • Estimates, not advice. A tool helps you understand your situation and prepare your questions. The agency's own decision is the one that counts.
  • Independent. LoopNode is not affiliated with any government agency and does not use any agency's name, logo or look as its own.

Why one small site per tool

People search for a specific answer — “does my car qualify for the loan interest deduction?”, “متى أتقاعد؟” — not for a toolbox. A site that does one thing can explain that one thing properly: what the rule says, a worked example, what the tool cannot know, and a short FAQ. It loads fast on a phone and reads naturally in its own language and direction.

This page is the front door: it lists every tool and holds the policies that apply to all of them.

Found a mistake?

Rules change and we can get things wrong. If a result looks off, tell us what you entered and what you expected, with a link to the official source if you have one. We correct errors quickly and update the check date on the page.
