Who we are

About LoopNode

LoopNode is made by Uzair Ali Murtaza, an independent developer in Lahore, Pakistan. Each tool lives on its own subdomain of loopnode.live and is written for the people who search for it, in their language.

What we care about

  • Showing the working. A number without its steps is hard to trust, so every result comes with the calculation and the rule behind it.
  • Primary sources, with dates. Rules are taken from the official pages that publish them and carry the date they were last checked.
  • Privacy by design. No accounts, no databases, nothing stored. Where a tool must ask an outside service — the car checker asks NHTSA to decode a VIN — it says so plainly.
  • Plain limits. Each tool lists what it cannot know and who gives the binding answer.

How a tool is checked before release

The rules are transcribed into a data file with the source link and check date for every row. Automated tests confirm that each quoted rule still appears on the saved copy of its source page, and that the tool reproduces the official worked examples. Pages are tested on phones and desktops, in light and dark mode, for accessibility, and to make sure nothing you type is sent anywhere it should not go.

Not affiliated

LoopNode is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any government agency, including the IRS, NHTSA and the General Organization for Social Insurance. Results are estimates to help you understand your situation, not tax, legal or financial advice.

Corrections

If you find an error, contact us. We fix confirmed errors and update the check date on the affected page.
