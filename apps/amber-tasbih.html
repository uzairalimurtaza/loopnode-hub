Amber Tasbih

Android · On Google Play

A tasbih counter for dhikr: count in rounds of 33 with a tap or the volume keys, fully offline.

What it does

Amber Tasbih is a counter for dhikr. Tap the large area in the middle of the screen to count, or press the volume up or down button while the counter is open. The count moves around a round of 33, like a misbaha, and each full round adds to a ×N marker for the day. If you miscount, a long-press takes one back.

Pick a target of 33, 100, 500 or 1000; when you reach it, the count starts again at 0. Seven preset dhikr are grouped as after prayer, istighfar, tahlil and salawat, and you can add your own, with optional Arabic text. The counter comes in four styles, Thread · Silk, Breath, Obsidian and Aurora Beads, with 59 colour sets whose colour shifts as you near your target.

Every count is saved on the phone as you go. A Stats tab shows a calendar heatmap, your streaks, your best day and the last 7 days against the 7 before. The screen stays on while you count, a short vibration marks each count, and you can switch on a daily reminder. Counting needs no connection and no account.

Who it is for

  • Muslims who recite the tasbih after salah: SubhanAllah 33, Alhamdulillah 33, Allahu Akbar 34
  • People counting istighfar, durood or their own wird toward 100, 500 or 1000
  • People who like to count by feel with the volume buttons
  • Arabic and Urdu speakers who want the whole app in their language, right to left
  • People who do dhikr at night, with true-black colour sets and the screen kept on

Features

  • Rounds of 33, with a ×N marker for each full round today
  • Targets of 33, 100, 500 or 1000; the count restarts at 0 when reached
  • Volume up or down counts too, while the counter is open
  • Tap the large centre area to count; long-press to take one back
  • A short vibration on each count; optional stronger buzz at 33
  • Optional chime when you finish a set; off by default
  • Four counter styles: Thread · Silk, Breath, Obsidian and Aurora Beads
  • 59 colour sets in 8 groups; the colour shifts as you near the target
  • 7 preset dhikr: after prayer, istighfar, tahlil and salawat
  • Add your own dhikr, with optional Arabic text
  • Every count is saved on the phone; long-press Reset clears, with Undo
  • Calendar heatmap for the last 30 days, 90 days or all time
  • Streaks, best day, average per day counted, last 7 days vs the 7 before
  • The screen stays on while you count (you can turn this off)
  • Optional daily reminder, off by default
  • Counts fully offline
  • No ads, no account, no tracking

Languages

English, Arabic and Urdu. Arabic and Urdu read right to left, and Urdu is set in Nastaliq type. On a phone set to another language, the app opens in English.

Privacy

Your counts, history and own dhikr stay on the phone. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Read the Amber Tasbih privacy policy.
